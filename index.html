Frequently Asked Questions

What is longarm quilting?

Longarm quilting is the process of using a large, specialized sewing machine to stitch together the three layers of a quilt — the top, batting, and backing. The long arm allows for smooth, consistent stitching across large quilt surfaces without the difficulty of maneuvering a big quilt through a regular sewing machine.

How do I prepare my quilt top for longarm quilting?

Press your quilt top well with all seams flat. Check for any loose threads or seams that need reinforcing. Make sure your backing is at least 4–6 inches larger on all sides than your quilt top. I'll walk you through specifics when you contact me!

What is your turnaround time?

Turnaround times vary by season and current workload. Typically, pantograph/edge-to-edge quilts are completed within 1–2 weeks, while custom quilts may take 2–4 weeks. Contact me to get an accurate estimate for your project.

How do you charge for your services?

Pricing is based on the square footage of the quilt and the complexity of the quilting design. Edge-to-edge pantographs start at a lower rate, while custom quilting is priced higher due to the additional time and artistry involved. Reach out for a personalized quote!

Do you provide thread and batting?

Yes! I carry a selection of quality thread colors and batting options. Just let me know your color preferences and desired loft when you contact me, and I'll let you know what I have available.
